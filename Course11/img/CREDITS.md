# Image credits — Course11

All figures in this folder are generated by `scripts/make_figures_c11.py`
(`make Course11/img/pipeline.png`). The heartbeats are the ECG200 training set of the
UCR time series archive (R. Olszewski, *Generalized feature extraction for structural
pattern recognition in time-series data*, PhD thesis, Carnegie Mellon University,
2001; <https://www.timeseriesclassification.com>), downloaded by
`Course11/temporal_signal.ipynb`. The other concept figures are synthetic and seeded;
`results.png` plots the numbers measured in `Course11/temporal_signal-solutions.ipynb`.

- `dilation.png` — dilated causal convolutions after A. van den Oord et al., *WaveNet:
  A Generative Model for Raw Audio*, 2016, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.03499>.
