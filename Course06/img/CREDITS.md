# Image credits — Course06

All figures in this folder are generated by `scripts/make_figures_c06.py`
(`make figures`) from synthetic, seeded data. Some reproduce ideas from published work:

- `crowded_valley.png` — summarises R. M. Schmidt, F. Schneider, P. Hennig,
  *Descending through a Crowded Valley — Benchmarking Deep Learning Optimizers*,
  ICML 2021, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01547>.
- `grad_clipping.png` — after I. Goodfellow, Y. Bengio, A. Courville, *Deep Learning*,
  MIT Press 2016, §10.11 (itself after Pascanu, Mikolov, Bengio, 2013).
- `dropout.png` — after N. Srivastava et al., *Dropout: A Simple Way to Prevent
  Neural Networks from Overfitting*, JMLR 2014.
- `softmax_why.png` — idea after A. Liusie, *Intuitively Understanding the Cross
  Entropy Loss* (2021), <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pwgpl9mKars>.
