# Image credits — Course10

All figures in this folder are generated by `scripts/make_figures_c10.py`
(`make figures`). The concept figures are synthetic and seeded; `results.png`,
`overlap.png` and `analogies.png` plot the numbers measured in
`Course10/rnn_next_word-solutions.ipynb`. Some reproduce ideas from published work:

- `vanishing.png`, `bptt.png` — after Y. Bengio, P. Simard, P. Frasconi, *Learning
  long-term dependencies with gradient descent is difficult*, IEEE Trans. Neural
  Networks 1994, and R. Pascanu, T. Mikolov, Y. Bengio, *On the difficulty of training
  recurrent neural networks*, ICML 2013, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.5063>.
- `vanishing_measured.png` — measured with the `MyRNN` code of the slides.
- `lstm_intuition.png`, `lstm_cell.png`, `lstm_equations.png` — the LSTM of
  S. Hochreiter, J. Schmidhuber, *Long Short-Term Memory*, Neural Computation 1997,
  with the forget gate of F. Gers, J. Schmidhuber, F. Cummins, *Learning to Forget:
  Continual Prediction with LSTM*, Neural Computation 2000; the cell diagram follows
  the layout popularised by C. Olah, *Understanding LSTM Networks*, 2015,
  <https://colah.github.io/posts/2015-08-Understanding-LSTMs/>.
- `lstm_to_gru.png` — comparison after J. Chung, C. Gulcehre, K. Cho, Y. Bengio,
  *Empirical Evaluation of Gated Recurrent Neural Networks on Sequence Modeling*, 2014,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.3555>.
- `gru.png` — equations of K. Cho et al., *Learning Phrase Representations using RNN
  Encoder–Decoder for Statistical Machine Translation*, EMNLP 2014,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.1078>.
- `cbow_recap.png`, `analogies.png` — after T. Mikolov, K. Chen, G. Corrado, J. Dean,
  *Efficient Estimation of Word Representations in Vector Space*, 2013,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.3781>; the analogy questions are its test set.
