# Image credits — Course09

The figures in this folder are generated by `scripts/make_figures_c09.py`
(`make figures`); they illustrate the normalization slides. The concept figures are
synthetic and seeded; `bn_results.png` plots the numbers measured in
`Course09/linear_vs_conv-solutions.ipynb` (without batch norm) and
`Course09/linear_vs_conv-norm.ipynb` (with batch norm). Some reproduce ideas from
published work:

- `bn_equations.png`, `bn_example.png`, `bn_train_eval.png`, `covariate_shift.png` —
  after S. Ioffe, C. Szegedy, *Batch Normalization: Accelerating Deep Network Training
  by Reducing Internal Covariate Shift*, ICML 2015, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03167>.
- `norm_axes.png` — layout after Y. Wu, K. He, *Group Normalization*, ECCV 2018,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.08494>.
